AV Autobody

Complete Autobody Repairs, logo & marketing collateral

Sector
Automotive repair
Role
Brand designer
Scope
Logo, look & feel
Touchpoints
Print, digital, apparel

Brief 01

This brand was created for a small business that offers car body repair services. The client asked for the letters “AV” to be incorporated into the mark.

Solution 02

The design was done to look minimalistic, folding the letters AV into a car silhouette with a spray gun. The concept was proposed and approved.

Execution 03

I then created a look and feel, and a few marketing collaterals for digital and print use: a services flyer, apparel and stationery.
