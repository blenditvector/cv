Ginger Me

Homemade Ginger Beer Drink, brand identity & label system

Sector
FMCG Beverage
Role
Brand Designer
Scope
Logo, label, brand system
Touchpoints
Packaging, marketing

Problem 01

Ginger Me required a distinct visual identity to position the brand as a homemade, natural ginger-based beverage while standing out in a crowded FMCG shelf environment.

Strategy 02

The design strategy focused on establishing immediate shelf impact while communicating authenticity and product strength, using bold typography, natural color cues, and high contrast visual elements.

Design System 03

A modular brand system was established, defining typography hierarchy, color application rules, and layout principles to ensure consistent execution across packaging and marketing touchpoints.

Solution 04

I designed the brand's visual identity, taking ownership of logo creation, label design, and the creation of a structured system to support future growth.

Execution 05

I designed the system's implementation for its bottle labels. I also established templates and usage standards to ensure brand consistency at scale for other flavours.

Outcome 06

The identity improved shelf presence, strengthened brand recognition, and enabled faster rollout of additional flavors and promotional materials.
